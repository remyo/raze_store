# Data and image attribution

Product metadata in this catalog pack is based on contributions to Open Food
Facts and is reused under the Open Database License 1.0. Individual database
contents are under the Database Contents License 1.0.

The bundled product images are based on contributions to Open Food Facts and
are reused at 256 px under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0. The
source product page and original image URL are retained on each record in
`catalog.json`. Package artwork and trademarks may be subject to additional
third-party rights.

Open Food Facts: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/

Three historical reference prices identify their exact source and effective
date in `catalog.json`. They come from the Philippines Department of Trade and
Industry BNPC SRP Bulletin dated 1 February 2025. They are suggestions only;
the price charged by the store remains editable and authoritative.
